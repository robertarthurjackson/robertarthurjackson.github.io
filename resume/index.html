Summary

Naturopathic doctor with more than a decade of clinical practice in pain management, interventional orthopaedics and regenerative medicine, mental health, and functional medicine. Founder of the Calgary Pain Centre. President and Chairman of the Board of the College of Naturopathic Doctors of Alberta since 2022, where I have led an overhaul of the regulator’s governance and finances and a three-year effort with the Alberta health ministry to expand the profession’s scope of practice. Fluent in bitcoin on-chain analytics, with a standing interest in economics, markets, and history.
